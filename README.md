# Autonomous Product Recall & Traceability Agent

## Overview

An agentic AI system that autonomously investigates product recall events,
traces affected products across the supply chain, performs impact analysis,
coordinates containment and recall actions, verifies execution, and maintains
an auditable decision trail.

## Problem

Product recalls require rapid identification of affected batches,
suppliers, warehouses, shipments and distributors. These relationships
are often distributed across multiple operational records, making manual
investigation slow and difficult to verify.

## Solution

Our multi-agent system uses specialized AI agents to:

- Detect recall triggers
- Trace product genealogy
- Analyze recall impact
- Obtain human approval
- Execute containment workflows
- Coordinate communications
- Plan reverse logistics
- Verify recall completion
- Maintain an audit trail
